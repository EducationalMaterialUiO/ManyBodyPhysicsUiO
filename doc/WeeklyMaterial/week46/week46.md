## Plan for week 46

### Thursday November 12, 2 × 45 min: the linked-diagram theorem
- Lecture 1: the RS series with the original perturbation and after removing the reference scalar (W = H_I − E⁽¹⁾ I, with ε₀ + E⁽¹⁾ staying in the physical energy); the wave operator drawn with bare vertices – connected, disconnected-but-linked (products of amplitudes: the quadruple of Ψ⁽²⁾ is t_m t_n) and unlinked (a closed first-order piece floating beside an open one) diagrams at second and third order; closing with a vertex – connected open pieces give connected energy diagrams, disconnected open pieces can still give a connected energy diagram when one vertex closes all their lines (two singles closed by one interaction), detached closed pieces stay unlinked; the renormalisation terms, including the positive inclusion–exclusion terms, cancel the unlinked sum (pairing model: the unlinked piece is −0.0125868 at g = 0.5 and is cancelled by the folded contribution +0.0125868)
- Normal ordering removes the one-vertex closed pieces; the first surviving unlinked diagrams appear at fourth order, and the factorisation 1/(D_m(D_m+D_n)D_n) + 1/(D_m(D_m+D_n)D_m) = 1/(D_m² D_n) identifies the unlinked sum cancelled by the folded term −E⁽²⁾⟨W R₀² W⟩; two non-interacting copies as a check
- Lecture 2: the factorisation theorem for two open histories by induction, and the case where one component closes (boundary condition S₀q = 0 from Q; the five allowed interleavings for a two-vertex closed piece); the theorem for energy and wave operator; the proof in imaginary time (Z(T), Dyson series, connected pieces exponentiate, the marked vertex, intervals become denominators, vacuum pieces cancel in the wave operator) and why the long-time limit can be taken order by order in a finite basis with a gap; Pauli exclusion at an intermediate cut, direct and exchanged hole lines at fourth order, why the exclusion-principle-violating linked terms are kept; the two-level benchmark counted (+g⁴/Δ³ from EPV diagrams alone); Brillouin–Wigner denominators do not factorise; consequences for independent systems
